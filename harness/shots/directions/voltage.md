# Voltage — kinetic, stylised, action-forward direction
(Studied from the direction craft of stylised AI-animation creators Sam admires: the camera language, rhythm and colour.
Never their characters, worlds, scenes or adult content. Everything here is original and brand-safe.)

LOOK
- Stylised painterly 3D with an anime-illustration finish: cel-like shading on sculpted 3D forms, hand-painted textures,
  crisp rim light, rich ambient occlusion. Characters expressive and appealing, never sexualised; full clothing.
- Bold complementary palettes: saturated orange vs teal, crimson vs deep blue, warm sun vs cold shadow. Deep blacks.
- Atmosphere everywhere: god rays through gaps, dust motes, sparks, water spray frozen mid-air, smoke.

LENS AND ANGLE
- Ultra-wide (14–18 mm) right up close: faces, hands and tools fill the frame with forced perspective and slight barrel distortion.
- Extreme low angles looking up; occasional top-down; Dutch tilts on tension beats.
- Foreground clutter framing the subject (tools, cables, props, glass edges) for depth; layered planes.
- Hero inserts: macro on the decisive detail (a screw biting, a switch flicking, a splash).

MOVEMENT
- Every shot moves with intent: crash zoom, fast push-in, orbit around the subject, whip pan out, handheld energy on action.
- Peak moments in slow motion (speed "slow"), then snap back to real time on the next cut.

EDIT
- Fast and musical: action cuts 0.8–2 s, breathers 3–4 s; cuts land on the beat.
- Transitions: hard cuts and smash cuts by default; "whip" between locations; "flash" on impacts. Dissolves almost never.
- Structure: cold-open hook (an impossible-looking angle) → escalating montage → one slow-motion peak → calm hero shot → end card.

SOUND
- Punchy modern score (hip-hop/electronic breakbeat or cinematic trailer percussion), 100–128 BPM, a drop on the peak.
- Voiceover sparse, confident, a smile in the voice; 2–3 lines max in 20–30 s.
