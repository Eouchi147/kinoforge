# Flash Editorial — modern food-magazine and social style (AI stills on colour + code type)
LOOK: hard direct flash, crisp shadows, saturated flat colour backdrops (one bold colour per shot), graphic top-down and 45-degree compositions, empty space left for type. Photoreal, never illustrated.
MOVEMENT: mostly stills brought to life in code: snap zooms, slides, stop-motion swaps of objects, colour-field cuts on the beat. Light AI motion only (a drip, a steam curl).
PEOPLE: none, or a hand placing an object.
EDIT: very fast, 0.4-1 s per beat; big kinetic type on the colour fields; end card in code.
SOUND: upbeat, percussive, 110-125 BPM; short punchy voiceover or none.
WORKS FOR: restaurants and cafes, beauty products, barbers' products; strong for 9:16 social.
