# Freeze Frame — high-speed studio advertising (AI, photoreal, no people)
LOOK: ingredients or tools suspended in mid-air, frozen splashes and dust, every particle sharp. Seamless dark or brand-colour backdrop, hard rim light, soft front fill. Phantom high-speed camera aesthetic.
MOVEMENT: ultra slow motion drifts and slow orbits around the frozen moment; a burst then a hold.
PEOPLE: none.
EDIT: few shots (3-5), each a held "wow" moment; kinetic type set in code between them.
SOUND: impact hits, whooshes, a held low drone; voiceover sparse.
WORKS FOR: restaurants (spices, rice, tea splash), detailing (water and foam), barbers (talc, water mist, comb and scissors).
