# Documentary — real people at work, film look (AI with people: riskiest, check every frame)
LOOK: candid and unposed, 35 mm film (Kodak Portra), natural grain, practical and window light, honest skin texture, shallow depth of field.
MOVEMENT: handheld but steady, slow observational moves, rack focus to hands at work.
PEOPLE: the owner and staff at work; modest wardrobe; no talking to camera unless it is a real person with written consent.
EDIT: 2-4 s shots, cuts on action; quiet voiceover; type and end card in code.
SOUND: room tone and real work sounds first; sparse piano or guitar.
NOTE: best with the client's own footage; AI people need a frame-by-frame check for hands and faces before any video money.
