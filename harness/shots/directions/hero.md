# Hero Close-ups — premium product and food cinematography (AI, photoreal, no people)
WHY: AI video is at its best on materials (crust, char, steam, liquid, paint, chrome) and at its worst on faces and hands. This direction keeps the camera on the product.
LOOK: photographed for real, never illustrated. Dark set, one hard key or backlight, deep blacks, true colours, steam and smoke backlit, crisp texture, subtle film grain. ARRI Alexa 35 look.
LENS: 85-100 mm macro, f/2-f/4, very shallow focus; grill-level and table-level angles; occasional top-down.
MOVEMENT: slow push-ins and slider moves, rack focus, high-speed slow motion on pours, flares and drops. Nothing shaky, nothing morphing.
PEOPLE: none. Hands only when unavoidable, out of focus, never the subject.
EDIT: 1.5-3 s shots cut on the beat; type and brand set in code between or over shots; end card in code.
SOUND: tight modern score 90-110 BPM with real foley (sizzle, crackle, pour); voiceover warm and few words.
WORKS FOR: restaurants (bread, grill, tea), detailing (water beading, foam, polish), barbers (razor, hot towel, pomade).
