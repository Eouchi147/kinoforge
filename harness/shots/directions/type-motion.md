# Type in Motion — kinetic typography and graphic motion design (code-built, no AI fees)
LOOK: the brand's own colours as full-frame fields, one bold variable typeface (width and weight animate), simple graphic shapes for the product, photos only inside letters or masks.
MOVEMENT: words slam and rise out of masks on the beat, letters stretch and breathe, shapes grow into the next background (match cuts), wipes on the beat.
RENDER: ads/board/type-motion (HTML/CSS stepped frame by frame by harness/dom-render.cjs). Exact, sharp, no AI glitches, free to render.
EDIT: 0.5 s beat grid; 3-5 scenes of one line each; end card with the logo drawn in.
SOUND: modern percussive score with hits on every cut; optional voiceover reading the lines.
WORKS FOR: every industry; strongest as the opening and closing of an ad, or alone for offers and announcements.
