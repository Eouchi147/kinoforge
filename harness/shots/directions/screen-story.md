# Screen Story — product UI motion design on a phone (code-built, no AI fees)
LOOK: a real phone with the actual product screens (chat, notifications, lock screen), soft studio background in brand colours, large clean type beside it. Apple and Stripe launch-film language.
MOVEMENT: the phone floats in 3D and turns between scenes; messages type and pop; the camera leans in on the key moment; the end card grows out of the phone.
RENDER: ads/board/screen-story (HTML/CSS stepped frame by frame by harness/dom-render.cjs). Exact UI, readable text, free to render.
EDIT: one claim per scene, 2-3 s each; logo drawn on the end card.
SOUND: clean minimal electronic score, UI sounds (send, receive, notification), calm voiceover.
WORKS FOR: Unmissed's own ads (shows the product working); a client's online ordering, booking or enquiry flow.
