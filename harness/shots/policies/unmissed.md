# Unmissed policy. The director follows it in every Unmissed piece.
# Sources: the Unmissed chat's answers (15 Oct 2026) and the Unmissed site config of 15 Oct 2026 (Eouchi147/sovereign-studio,
# site.config.js and tools/check.mjs, branch claude/focused-einstein-f9sq5v). When they disagree, the site config wins: re-read it first.

VOICE AND TEXT
- Plain, warm, direct, short sentences, no hype. The brand speaks as a team ("we"), never a first name.
- Never use em dashes, en dashes or "..." in voiceover, end cards or any on-screen text.
- No prices, no URLs (the domain is not live yet; never the vercel.app address), no competitor names.
- No old plan names ("Never Miss a Call", "Website Rebuild"), no founding offers, no "free proof build", no "pay after", no
  December launch or reservation list (phone answering is live), not the old business name.

OFFER (15 Oct 2026): three plans, Presence (growing businesses), AI Front Desk (established businesses), Complete (both).
- Tagline: "A premium presence. An AI front desk." Second line: "Built for you, run for you, paid monthly."
- Hero line: "Your business is losing customers in the gaps." Audience: owner-run businesses in Canada.

CLAIMS (Unmissed's own pieces), allowed only as the site words them:
- an AI receptionist answers every call, day and night, in the business's name; it says it is an AI and that the call is recorded
- books into the owner's calendar; a summary of every call sent to the owner as soon as it ends
- website chat and text messages answered in seconds, day and night; a missed call texted back in seconds
- every new lead followed up, politely and only where the law allows; the owner alerted on their phone
- every customer marked done asked for a Google review
- a premium website, the Google Business Profile run for them, social posts; English or French
- the owner owns the domain, content and customer list; a free website audit: 26 checks in about 20 seconds
NOT allowed: testimonials, ratings, review or client counts, client logos, results or ROI figures, "trusted by", "#1", rankings,
"most popular", multipliers ("10x"), urgency or scarcity, discounts, absolute promises beyond the site's words, an AI voice or face
passed off as a real person, real people without written consent.
Demos use invented businesses and say so on screen ("Demo with an invented business").

CLIENT AND CONCEPT ADS
- A client's ad: only claims the client can prove in writing (stars, years, licences), checked by Unmissed first.
- Concept ads built for prospects are labelled "Concept by Unmissed, AI-assisted". The free proof build is retired (the free
  audit is the only free thing), so whether concept ads are free, paid or a plan feature is for the Unmissed chat to decide.
- Industries now: restaurants and caterers (no alcohol, no pork on screen; Ramadan/Eid hooks welcome), auto repair and detailing,
  barbers and beauty salons (non-medical only). No health clinics. Real estate later.
- Defaults: modest wardrobe for every person on screen. A no-music cut (voice and sound design only) when asked.

BRAND (Unmissed's own pieces)
- Look: the site's night theme by default: background #05070f with a glow of #0b1530 / #070c1c, ink #f4f6fb, secondary ink
  #c3cad8 and #98a1b5, accent #79b4ff, action blue #2f7df6, deep blue #0b3b8f, violet #8c6eff in the mark only. The hero is a glass
  ring ("the catch"). The site also has a day theme (#f4f6fa, ink #0b1020, accent #2456c9).
- Type: Instrument Sans 400-700 (JetBrains Mono for small labels). Logo: a ring with a small node at about 1 o'clock
  (ring #2f7df6, node #79b4ff), drawn in code.
- Unmissed pieces say "Made with AI, checked by a person".

LOOKS: photoreal or code-built motion design only. No stylised 3D, cartoon or anime looks. Sam picks the direction from a style board
  (stills) before any video money is spent. Prefer shots without people.

ALWAYS: the platform AI label on realistic AI content; Sam approves before anything is sent or published.
Client material (briefs, photos, logos, finished client ads) never goes into the public kinoforge repo or its releases (parked until paying clients).
