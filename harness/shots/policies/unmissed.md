# Unmissed policy (from the Unmissed chat, 15 Oct 2026). The director follows it in every Unmissed piece.

VOICE AND TEXT
- Plain, warm, direct, short sentences, no hype. The brand speaks as a team ("we"), never a first name.
- Never use em dashes, en dashes or "..." in voiceover, end cards or any on-screen text.
- No prices, no URLs (none exists yet; never the vercel.app address), no competitor names.

CLAIMS (Unmissed's own pieces) — allowed only as worded:
- every website enquiry answered in seconds, day and night, signed by the business
- the owner alerted on their phone, and one polite follow-up
- every customer marked done asked for a review within 24 hours
- English and French
- the owner owns every account and approves what goes live
- a free website audit: 26 checks in about 20 seconds
- phone answering only as "taking orders now, live from December 2026"
NOT allowed: testimonials, ratings, review or client counts, client logos, results or ROI figures, "trusted by", "#1", rankings,
absolute promises ("never miss a call again"), fake urgency, an AI voice or face passed off as a real person, real people.

CLIENT AND CONCEPT ADS
- A client's ad: only claims the client can prove in writing (stars, years, licences), checked by Unmissed first.
- Concept ads for prospects: built from public information only, labelled "Concept by Unmissed, AI-assisted".
- Industries now: restaurants and caterers (no alcohol, no pork on screen; Ramadan/Eid hooks welcome), auto repair and detailing,
  barbers and beauty salons (non-medical only). No health clinics. Real estate later.
- Defaults: modest wardrobe for every person on screen. A no-music cut (voice and sound design only) when asked.

BRAND (Unmissed's own pieces)
- Positioning: "The AI front desk for owner-run businesses." Tagline: "See it working. Then decide." Second line: "You own it. You approve it. We prove it."
- Colours: ink #101014, background #f4f5f7, white #ffffff, muted #565b66, action blue #2f5fd0; gradient #0b3b8f #2f7df6 #79b4ff; violet #8c6eff in the mark only.
- Type: Instrument Sans 400-700 (JetBrains Mono for small labels). Logo: a ring with a small node at about 1 o'clock (ring #2f7df6, node #79b4ff, on #101014), drawn in code.

LOOKS: photoreal or code-built motion design only. No stylised 3D, cartoon or anime looks. Sam picks the direction from a style board
  (stills) before any video money is spent. Prefer shots without people; AI people only in the documentary direction, checked frame by frame.

ALWAYS: the platform AI label on realistic AI content; Sam approves before anything is sent or published.
Client material (briefs, photos, logos, finished client ads) never goes into the public kinoforge repo or its releases.
