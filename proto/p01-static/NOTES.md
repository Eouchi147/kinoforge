# P01 — STATIC (10 s, 1280x720 carrying a 4:3 CRT picture, 24 fps, 240 f)

**Lane:** dark storytelling / analog horror ("Dark Corners"). **Logline (16 words):** A 1990s security tape of an empty school corridor at 2:14 AM; between frames, something is closer.
**Proves:** the kit's `vhs` mode as a complete analog-horror finish (4:3 CRT window, burned-in OSD that suffers the same tape faults as the picture), a scare built on a CONTINUITY CHANGE rather than a jump (R-8-07), deterministic glitch timing shared frame-for-frame between picture and sound, and that a scene can extend the post chain with its own passes without touching `film.js`.
**Emotion arc (Plutchik):** apprehension (S1 the clock skips a second) → surprise/apprehension (S2 the door is open) → fear (S3 something is standing in it) → void (S4) → lingering dread (S5 "this is tape 3 of 11").

The card reframes everything as one of eleven tapes (open loop, R-1-08).

## Look
**Set:** 3.2 x 3.0 x 15.4 m corridor; painted block walls with grime gathering low and high and a water stain; 5 locker banks (vents, latches, blank number plates, dents, kicks, rust) with sloped hoods and end caps; 4 classroom doors (one as a near-right foreground vertical); 2x2 ft drop ceiling with water stains and a return-air grille; waxed VCT floor (roughness 0.17–0.36 from a canvas map, grout and fbm grime darkening along the locker line, buffed traffic lane, heel scuffs); a glossy wet patch placed where P5 mirrors in it (so the puddle flickers with the tube); a sun-washed blank notice above the first door; bin; double-faced bracket clock; far fire door with push bar under an abstract red exit sign (no letters). Stairwell beyond: unlit set lit only by its own cold window (MeshBasic), placed by projecting the doorway from the camera so the window sits behind the figure's torso and head.
**Figure:** capsules and a sphere, albedo 0.02, ~2.2 m (head bent under the 2.12 m lintel = "slightly too tall"), arms to the knee and clear of the body, weight on one leg, faces the camera; ≥3 stops under the doorway behind it (R-4-04).
**Colour script:** S1/S2 sick fluorescent green-white highlights, teal-grey lockers, beige-green tile, milky green-cyan blacks; red exit sign + REC dot are the only warm points (<1% of frame). S2 after the band: a new cold blue-grey rectangle at the end of the hall. S3: the frame fills with the dim far end, P5 overhead, the red sign at top, the pale doorway, the black figure (darkest value in the piece). S4 true black. S5 dim grey type on tape-black.
**Grade (one):** kit `vhs` (mode 1: curvature, chroma bleed, rolling jitter line, scanlines) + saturation 0.70, contrast 1.06, grain 0.12 (grainSize 2) on every tape frame, vignette 0.40, halation 0.12 (highlights only), lift [0.020, 0.028, 0.026], green-cyan shadowTint; tone-mapping exposure 1.15; bloom 0.3 (threshold 0.92, only tubes and the exit sign). Weave and modeAmount spike only inside the tracking error. S4 is a true-black slug (as p05's S4).
**Post chain (scene-owned passes, kit untouched):** RenderPass → bloom → OutputPass → **zoom** (digital crop + bilinear upscale of the sensor image, slight CCD softness) → kit overlay (burned-in OSD) → **playback** (tracking band, line tearing, head-switching skew at the picture bottom, ground-loop hum bar, signal loss; these hit the OSD too, as on a real tape) → kit finish (`vhs`) → **bezel** (rounded 4:3 window, clean black pillars).

## Sound (audio.py prints the cue sheet; all times = frame / 24, same tables as scene.js)
Beds f0–209, trimmed so they are at full level when the tape stops: 60 Hz hum (−28), tape hiss (−34) with slow movement, P5 buzz (saturated 120 Hz, −36) gated by the FLICKER table with 3 ms edges, sub drone (lp 150) rising −40 → −26 dB. Ballast ticks on every flicker edge. Tracking error f87–104: hiss burst with 29 Hz head chatter (−27), brown rumble lp 120 (−26), crackle (−32). Thump f104 (hit, sub 38, no click, −18). Shepard f139–209, −32 → −20 dB. Dropout f173–175 ducked −60 dB (measured −81 dB RMS) then a click on f175. S4 f209–223 digital silence. Card tone f223–240. No music. Measured after `Mix.write`: 10.000 s (480000 samples, 48 kHz stereo), peak −2.44 dBFS; bed ≈ −22 dB RMS, band and thump ≈ 9 dB above it, S3 tail +3 dB (the build), card −26 dB.
R-3-06: the piece's impact is an absence (the hard cut to 14 f of digital silence), and the card tone follows it. The thump is quiet by design (dread, not a jump).

- **Speed** (1280x720): ≈1.9 s/frame steady state on a quiet machine (≈2.8 s under 3x CPU contention); a lone frame costs ~6.7 s because SwiftShader compiles all shaders on frame 1.
- **Kit bug found (worked around, not edited):** `makeComposer` tags the overlay CanvasTexture `SRGBColorSpace`, so it is decoded to linear on sampling but composited into the display-referred buffer (after OutputPass) and never re-encoded: drawn greys come out dark (measured 64→13, 128→55, 192→134; black/white unaffected), so mid-tone titles, glows and shadows render darker than drawn. Workaround here: `osd()` pre-encodes overlay greys. Kit fix: `tex.colorSpace = THREE.NoColorSpace` for the overlay texture.
- **Still weak / next:** the figure's silhouette is cleaner and more graphic than "unclear" against the window (could add a breath of haze in the doorway or interlace-comb its edges); the right hood wedge is a large flat shape in S3; the band's snow pattern is regular (vary streak lengths, add a brief vertical roll with the VBI bar); holds rely on grain for life (a slow AGC breathing on exposure would help); the open door may be noticed immediately rather than "a beat later" (could dim the stairwell window further in S2 and let it brighten slightly into S3).
