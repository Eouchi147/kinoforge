# P11 — KINETIC (9:16 typographic hook, 8 s, 24 fps, 192 f) — scene id `p11-kinetic`

**Lane:** edutainment / dark-history short-form hook. Zero characters, zero 3D: Canvas 2D typography drawn through `boot()` + the kit overlay, so the kit's finish pass (grain, vignette, tint) gives the one grade.
**Logline (24 words):** A sober hook in six typographic beats: the year 1628, a mayor executed for witchcraft, then the letter he smuggled to his daughter survives.
**Proves:** (1) a text-only hook can be directed like film: cut on the beat, hits on landings, a pattern interrupt, a real silence before the open loop; (2) 9:16 safe-zone discipline (R-2-08) that is *verified*, not eyeballed (`?safe=1` mask + `?audit=all` text-box audit over all 192 frames); (3) picture and sound share one source of truth (`timing.js` is imported by `scene.js` and parsed as JSON by `audio.py`), so sync is by construction.

Render: always `--w 720 --h 1280` (all sizes are H-relative, 1080x1920 scales exactly). Params: `--param safe=1` draws the platform-UI mask; `--param audit=all` logs every text box that leaves the safe rectangle on any frame. Frames are pure functions of the frame index (two independent launches give byte-identical PNGs).

## Emotion tags (Plutchik)
B1 anticipation (apprehension) · B2 fear (apprehension → fear), surprise on the hit · B3/B4 surprise (the interrupt) → sadness (pensiveness) · B5 sadness (grief) · B6 anticipation (open loop). Contrast alternates dark/light at the one cut.

## Colour script & grade
Ink-black card, cream type, ONE red mark (B2 underline) → cut → cream parchment, ink type, dark letter, ONE red mark (B5 underline) → cream label on the dark letter. Red is 0.52–0.65 % of the frame (cap 3 %).
Grade `none` + finish: grain 0.04, vignette 0.30, highlightTint [0.03, 0.015, 0] (slight warm highlights), halation 0 (cream paper is all highlight; halation would tint the whole page), no letterbox (9:16 is never letterboxed). Measured: dark card mean (14.1, 12.6, 12.1) vs #0e0d0b; cream paper mean (240.7, 231.3, 212.6) vs #efe6d2.

## Typography system
- Bebas Neue: year, verdict lines, label. Cormorant Garamond italic 500/600: dateline and the three written lines.
- Tracking is set per line (letterSpacing in em): year +0.01, "A MAYOR WAS" +0.02, "EXECUTED" 0, "FOR WITCHCRAFT" +0.04, label +0.20, dateline +0.02; the written lines are natural.
- Every line is optically aligned to the left axis (x = 0.11 W) from the measured left bearing of its first glyph, and fitted to the measure where the design calls for a block.
- Everything printed or written goes through ONE ink layer with a speckled dropout mask (up to 20 % of the ink), so both cards share a printed/inked material instead of vector-clean type.
- Safe rectangle (R-2-08): x 0.11–0.78 W, y 0.15–0.65 H.

## Deviations from the brief (and why)
1. "1628" is cap 0.16 H, not ≈ 0.22 H: at 0.22 H cap the four digits (≈ 0.9 W) cannot fit the 0.67 W safe measure. The slam still starts at 1.3x.
2. "PART 1 OF 3" is the bottom of the *safe* area (baseline 0.59 H, arrow to 0.643 H), not the literal bottom: R-2-08 forbids text below 65 % H. The dark letter below it continues to the frame bottom as decoration (not text).
3. B2 is set as three fitted lines; "FOR WITCHCRAFT" is the payoff line (smaller than EXECUTED on purpose: EXECUTED is the verdict, the reason is the reveal).
4. The only frames with text outside the safe rectangle are f0–2: the brief's own 1.3x slam of the year (the smear ghosts included). Every other frame, held or moving, is inside (audit, see QC).
5. Added to the brief's sound list: the 12-frame vacuum before the cut (R-3-06), room tone, ink sounds in B5, a mid-band layer on the heartbeat.

## QC log
**Iter 1 — first pass, one frame per beat + the all-frames audit.** Audit flagged the B2 underline overshooting the measure by ~2 px, the "hand." landing popping beyond the measure, and long landing transients (1.3x on ordinary words). `track()` double-counted the shake for ink-layer draws. Fix: landing scales cut to 1.14 / 1.17 / 1.04, underline x1 = ink width, T3 fitted to 0.975 of the measure, shake subtracted only for the main ctx. Hierarchy decided: fit-to-measure verdict block, T1 as the thesis, T2/T3 supporting, one red mark per card.
**Iter 2 — surfaces.** The 70-scale fbm gave blocky blotches on the paper and the cream read 10–20 levels too dark; dark card too bright. Fix: fine tooth (scale 330) + very slow mottling, base brightened, dark base lowered. The 5 % ghost year behind B2 read as mud: removed.
**Iter 3 — the letter and legibility.** The letter read as a flat paper bag. Fix: warm top light on the tear, tilt −2.9°, dog-ear, trifold creases, faint script lines. At 180x320 the label and arrow were the weakest: enlarged (Bebas 0.042 H, arrow 0.035 H) and the dateline made larger and brighter. Stack spacing T1 → T2 → T3 → underline → torn edge checked.
**Iter 4 — motion + detail.** (a) The script strokes were regular sine waves: replaced by pen-like cursive (humps, ascender loops, descenders, downstroke weight, ragged right margin; abstract, not glyphs, R-9-07). (b) The 7-pose `lighter` smear stair-stepped on the torn edge; 17 poses tinted the dark sheet purple/green (8-bit rounding of 1/17 contributions); fixed by accumulating in float with the pose count following the smear length (3–32). (c) The first frame after the cut ghosted a half-transparent sheet (the shutter sampled an off-screen pose): fixed. (d) Arrow retimed so the finished state holds 4 f (f188–191).
**Iter 5 — safe zones while moving.** The all-frames audit still flagged the uniform scale-down landings of WAS / EXECUTED / FOR WITCHCRAFT (they end on the right edge of the measure, so any growth leaves the rectangle for 2–3 frames). Changed every B2 landing to a vertical-only stretch; the audit now flags only the year's briefed 1.3x slam.
**Final measurements.** Safe mask on f10 / f33 / f71 / f99 / f129 / f191: all text inside the cyan rectangle (`qc/safe_mask.jpg`). Audit over 192 frames (20 tracked boxes): only the year and its smear ghosts leave the rectangle, on f0–2. 180x320 test (`qc/lowres_180x320.png`): every line legible; the small italic T2/T3 lines are the weakest. Motion bursts read: slam f0–11 (undershoot, smear, rule, dateline), B2 landings f39–61, letter dip + outBack f72–81, T2 wipe f129–135, underline + bleed f154–169, label + arrow f178–191. Render speed at 720x1280 on the shared CPU: 1.1–1.5 s/frame for ordinary frames, 1.7–2.3 s on the motion-blurred letter frames (PNG encode included).

## Kit notes (worked around, not edited)
- `sfx.heartbeat()` raises `ValueError` (shape mismatch) when its last dub (k + 0.3·period + 0.25 s) overruns the buffer; its loop guard is `k < dur − 0.4`. Worked around by rendering long and trimming.
- `fbmCanvas` at mid frequencies (scale ≈ 70) gives blocky blotches; used fine-scale tooth plus a very slow layer instead.
- `Mix.put(..., db=)` shadows the module-level `db()` helper inside `put` (harmless, but `from sfx import *` users cannot call `db()` there).
- Kit finish `halation` tints a uniformly bright cream page; set to 0 here.
- `Mix.duck()` ramps back up *starting at* `end`, so a hit placed exactly at `end` loses its attack; release it `ramp` seconds early (done here).
