# P04 — WITNESS (6.0 s, 16:9, 24 fps, Blender Cycles CPU — `blender.py`, not Three.js)

**Lane:** dark storytelling / historical drama — the "cinema" look: path-traced practicals, a thin volume, real optical depth of field.
**Logline (≤25 words):** 1628, a study at night. A quill stops mid-word; the writer turns to the window; torchlight is coming up the lane toward his house.
**Proves:** the Blender lane of the contract (SHOTS/SETTINGS/build/frame) with motivated practical light only (candle 1800 K, pitch torches 1650 K through a leaded window, cool moonlit-sky fill), a homogeneous volume that the torchlight fills, an optical rack focus done in diopters, procedural materials with age (plaster grime/soot, laid paper with chain lines, oak, wool sheen, SSS tallow and skin, wavy crown glass, lead cames), deterministic animation with anticipation/overshoot/settle, camera placement *solved* from frame targets, and the one-grade film finish (halation, vignette, seeded grain) applied inside the same contract by a render_write handler.
**Emotion arc (Plutchik):** serenity/concentration → apprehension (the quill stops) → fear (the turn; the light arrives).

## Geography (overhead)
Writer near the origin facing +Y (north) at a heavy oak desk; a closed leather ledger with the sheet on it in front of him, written pages stacked to his left under his resting left hand, inkpot to his right, pewter chamberstick front-right with a burned-down tallow stub. Leaded two-light window (diamond quarries, saddle bars, oak frame and mullion) in the timber-framed north wall, front-left of him (head turn ≈ 40° left, 9° up from the window's centre; ≈ 50° from the page). A shelf of books and a jug on the wall between the window and the post behind him. Outside: a lane running almost due north (8° west) from the window, jettied facades, doorways, wet cobbles; the torches come straight down it. S1 camera sits at his left, low over the desk, looking front-right through the nib at the flame; S2 camera stands behind his left shoulder — both on his left (180° line kept). Eye-trace across the cut: flame (upper-left third of S1) → lit window (left third of S2).

## Colour script
S1: amber candle + cream paper on near-black; the flame is the one hot accent. → S2 opens dark: blue-black room (cool sky fill + deep-shadow tint), warm plaster pool only from the hidden candle, torches small and distant in the glass → orange spreads: brighter glass, torchlit fog, orange edge on his turned face → last frame: the lit window and his lit profile. Accent reserved for meaning: torch orange = the threat arriving (it only enters through the window).

## Grade (one for the whole clip)
AgX, look "AgX - Medium High Contrast", exposure −0.3, **camera white balance 2800 K** (tungsten-balanced stock: the 1800 K candle reads amber not red, the 9000 K sky fill reads blue). Film finish in `blender.py` (render_write handler, deterministic, same on every frame): halation on highlights only (luma > 0.8, orange, 0.18), a slight blue lift in the deepest shadows, vignette 0.28 (≈ 0.47 stop at the corners), grain 0.03 luma-weighted + a little chroma, seeded by frame index. No letterbox.

## Render settings and cost
Cycles CPU, adaptive (threshold 0.02), OIDN, max bounces 6 (diffuse 3, glossy 3, transmission 6, transparent 8), volume bounces 1, clamp indirect 8, caustics off, blur glossy 1, light tree, persistent data. Fog is one homogeneous box (density 0.05, anisotropy 0.3; distance-sampled, no ray marching), hidden in S1. Window glass: thin-walled rough glass for camera rays, transparent for shadow rays (so torchlight enters without caustics noise).
Profiling (CPU-seconds, 160x90/16 spp, S2 frame): base 4.1 s; without the room fog 2.6 s (fog ≈ 37 %); lane fog was ≈ 10 % (removed); outside geometry ≈ 15 %; materials ≈ 20 %; SSS, glass, ink curves, metaballs, subsurf each negligible.
Last pass trimmed noise/wave octaves where they are invisible and glossy bounces 3 → 2 (−12 % CPU, no visible change).
**Measured on the shared 2-core box** (other agents rendering; wall times inflated, CPU-seconds are the honest number):
- 640x360 / 24 spp, final code (load ≈ 2.5): S1 38–46 s/frame wall, S2 38–73 s/frame wall (≈ 50–60 CPU-s S1, S2 ≈ 1.3–1.7× that). Under load 5–6 earlier: S1 52–63 s, S2 89–123 s.
- **1280x720 / 96 spp, frame 0 (S1), final code: 508.8 s wall = 722 CPU-s** (before the trim: 614 s / 819 CPU-s).
- CI estimate (4 dedicated vCPU, same per-core speed): S1 ≈ 180 s/frame at 96 spp, ≈ 125–135 s at 64 spp; S2 ≈ 1.3–1.7× → ≈ 170–230 s at 64 spp. **Whole clip at 64 spp ≈ 6–7 h of runner time: does not fit one 45-min job** → split with --start/--end (≈ 15 frames per job), or drop to 24–32 spp (the 640x360/24 spp checks are clean after OIDN; ≈ 2.5 h). Far above the ~15 s/frame target at any of these settings; SETTINGS samples = 64 as instructed.
- Audio: 6.000 s, 48 kHz stereo, peak −2.66 dBFS; 17 f of near-silence 27.3 dB under the hit on the cut.
