# KINOFORGE in ten minutes (the human guide)

KINOFORGE is Sam's film studio run by AI agents. It makes films, ads and loops in code and with free tools, renders them
on free GitHub machines and on Sam's Mac, and since October 2026 it is the content engine of Unmissed, his marketing
agency. Sam decides; agents build, check and deliver. This page is for a person. Agents read `AGENTS.md` and the full
manual in `docs/manual/`.

## What it makes
- **Cutaway**: a block of the world cut open, its layers and hidden finds in view. The house grammar since 23 Oct 2026 is
  the seasons loop: one fixed view, no cuts, a living world changing in front of you. Twelve art directions can dress it
  (Fable, Night glow, Stained glass, Papercraft, Claymation, Pixel art, Risograph, Storybook ink, Blueprint, Chalkboard,
  Engraving, and a 2D Flat vector version).
- **Battle Map**: famous battles on a living strategy-game map, with a legend and planned cameras (eight films).
- **Dark Corners**: short history films in hand-built styles (Kinetic type, Lantern Road, Clay).
- **NOOR**: Ramadan samples under strict depiction rules.
- **Business ads and social videos** for Unmissed and its clients.

## How a film gets made
1. **Brief.** Sam (or the studio app) says what the film is: subject, style, format, length, music, budget cap.
2. **Script and plan.** The director pipeline writes the story and checks the facts; for Cutaway and Battle Map it writes a
   spec (data, not code) that the engine turns into pictures.
3. **Look.** Stills are rendered and looked at before anything long is rendered.
4. **Sound.** Free score engine (orchestral samples, public domain), CC0 nature and effects recordings, public-domain
   classical recordings, narration when a film needs a voice.
5. **Render.** Free GitHub runners (many machines in parallel) or Sam's Mac (fast, one machine, frames on the Samsung X5
   drive).
6. **Check.** Contact sheet, loudness, no frozen frames, words readable, house rules (no long dashes, words set in code).
7. **Deliver.** To Sam's Mac (`Video Production/kino-films/`), to the studio app's Library, and to GitHub releases.

## The rules that never bend
- Money: a hard cap per film; pay only for results that pass the checks; paid image or video generation needs Sam's go.
- Words, logos and numbers are set in code, never drawn by an AI image or video model.
- Honesty: facts checked with sources, demos say so on screen, AI content labelled where platforms ask.
- The repos are public: no secrets, no client material, nothing personal.
- Sam approves every look before production and everything before it is published or sent.

## What Sam does (and only this)
- Picks a direction or a look when asked (one short question, a recommendation first).
- Watches what lands and says yes, or what to change (in the studio's Library: Approve, Request changes, or a note pinned
  to a second of the film).
- Adds credit or keys himself when a paid step is wanted (agents never see keys).
