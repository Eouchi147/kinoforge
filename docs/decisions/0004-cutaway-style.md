- **Context:** Sam brought three reference pictures (idea only) of a block of land cut open. The first board "looked
  amazing"; he asked for cinema lighting, animated fauna and flora, easter eggs in the soil, interesting angles; then for

- **Consequences:** Blender renders are heavy (6 to 50 min per 1920 still on a runner): check with a few stills first,
