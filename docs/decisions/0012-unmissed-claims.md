# 0012 Unmissed and client claims only in their own written words

- **Date:** 15 Oct 2026. **Decided by:** Sam through the Unmissed chat.
- **Context:** Ads make promises. A false or inflated claim harms the client and Unmissed.
- **Decision:** Unmissed's own pieces use only claims worded as on its site (source of truth: `site.config.js` in
  `Eouchi147/sovereign-studio`; copy in `harness/shots/policies/unmissed.md`). No testimonials, ratings, client counts,
  results, prices, rankings, urgency or discounts on screen. Demos with invented businesses say so on screen. Realistic AI
  content carries the platform's AI label; Unmissed pieces say "Made with AI, checked by a person". A client's ad uses
  only claims the client can prove in writing. Halal-friendly restaurants: no alcohol, no pork, nothing that looks like
  either. Modest wardrobe.
- **Status:** Active. Read `harness/shots/policies/unmissed.md` before any Unmissed or client piece.
