Type in Motion Built in code

$0 to $0.50 per ad

Bold words in the restaurant's own colours, moving on the beat. Round 2: the bread is a real photo with its background removed. It drops onto the table, settles and breathes, with steam rising. The dots are now real tea glasses, sliding apart to make room for one more. Sound comes later; this preview is silent.

Best for
Every industry. Offers, openings, announcements, and the first and last seconds of any ad.
Watch out
On its own it shows little food. Strongest mixed with Hero Close-ups.

Screen Story Built in code

$0 per ad

The product working on a real phone: a late-night catering enquiry answered in seconds, the owner alerted, English and French. The natural look for Unmissed's own ad.

Best for
Unmissed. Any client with online booking, ordering or enquiries to show.
Watch out
Needs real screens to show. Says little about food or craft on its own.

Hero Close-ups

About $3 to $4 per ad

Dark, premium food photography with no people. Bread, fire and steam up close. AI video handles materials like these far better than faces and hands.

Best for
Restaurants. Detailing: water beading on paint, foam, polish. Barbers: razor, hot towel, pomade.
Watch out
Low risk. Every clip still gets checked for melting shapes before it goes in.

Freeze Frame

About $2 to $3 per ad

High-speed studio shots: ingredients bursting and frozen in mid-air, then a slow drift around the moment. Round 2: two 6 second clips made from the stills, $0.60 each.

Best for
Short, dramatic moments in any ad: spices, a tea splash, car foam, barber tools.
Watch out
Very dramatic. Best as two or three bursts, not a whole ad.

Flash Editorial

About $1 to $2 per ad

Hard-flash food on bold colour backgrounds, like a modern food magazine. Mostly still photos moved and cut in code, very fast.

Best for
Instagram and TikTok (9:16), cafes and restaurants, beauty products.
Watch out
Feels social and trendy rather than cinematic.

Every still was made from a written brief by AI image models. The words, logo and end cards are set in code, never drawn by AI. Concept pieces carry "Concept by Unmissed, AI-assisted".
