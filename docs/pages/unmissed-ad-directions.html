Five directions for the Unmissed ad

Same message, five ways to make it. Each one was built music first: every cut and word lands on the beat. Watch with sound, then tell me which one (or which two) to take to final.

All five: 16 to 27 seconds, 16:9. Voices were auditioned by an audio AI and the best take kept. These previews are 720p; the 1080p masters are in the repo's "ads" release.

What happens after you pick: I polish that one (sound balance, any timing you want changed), then make the 9:16 phone version laid out for Reels and TikTok, and a French version if you want it.

1 of 5, 16 s

Manifesto

Huge kinetic words, music-led. Four cold lines (9:47 PM, the phone rings, nobody picks up), then the drop: one word per beat. Voice only on the sign-off.

Feels
Bold, fast, Nike-style.
Best for
Reels and TikTok hooks, sound-off viewing.
Weak spot
Says little about how it works.
2 of 5, 18 s

The Catch

3D. Calls, messages and leads stream through dark space; some slip through the gaps until the glass ring forms and catches every one. The ring becomes the logo, like the site's hero.

Feels
Premium tech launch, calm and cinematic.
Best for
The brand film, YouTube, the site.
Weak spot
Abstract: no business shown.
3 of 5, 27 s

The Call

You hear a real-sounding late-night call between a customer and the AI front desk, and read it as giant captions, word by word. It books the job on the call. Then three supers on the beat.

Feels
Proof, not a pitch. "See it working."
Best for
Converting owners who doubt AI can handle a call.
Weak spot
The longest; slower start.
4 of 5, 23 s

Night Shift

A short film. An empty restaurant after closing, rain, a phone lighting up on the counter. "You closed at nine. Your customers didn't." Answered, booked, then morning light. Real-looking AI footage.

Feels
Emotional, cinematic, quiet.
Best for
Restaurants and any owner who goes home tired.
Weak spot
Costs about $2 in footage per new version.
5 of 5, 18 s

Bento

Bright, Apple-style. A grid of live tiles (calls answered, leads followed up, reviews asked for, the website, the Google profile) with the camera whipping between them on the beat.

Feels
Clean, optimistic, product launch.
Best for
Explaining everything the plans include.
Weak spot
Lots on screen; small text on phones.
