# Battle set piece: the mud at Agincourt (Cutaway inserts for the Battle Map film)

Two inserts cut into "The Battle of Agincourt" (`films/battles/agincourt/`, cues.json; the narration timing never
changes). Kid-safe: no wounds; the humour is in the mud and the timing.

| Insert | Cue span | Picture | Job |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | l06 to l07 (57.3 to 71.2 s): "It had rained all night. The field was now deep, sticky mud." / archers hammer stakes | a block of the freshly ploughed field in rain: furrows on top, the cut face shows soaked, dark, layered soil; rain puddles in the furrows; then sharpened stakes are driven in at an angle along the English edge, their points toward the French | the viewer sees why the field is a trap before anyone moves |
| B | l10 to l11 (86.7 to 100.3 s): "Then the French soldiers advanced on foot. Wearing armour. Through mud. Up to their knees." / squeezed by the woods | the same block, now with a line of small armoured figures walking from the far side; through the cut face we see their legs sink to the knee into the soft layer with each step, slower and slower; woods on both edges close in so the line bunches | the mud and the woods together are the turn of the battle |

Camera: A opens high and wide (the field as a map), pushes down to the cut face as the stakes go in. B starts low at the
cut face (the legs sinking), then rises to show the whole squeezed line. Overcast light, rain in A, grey after in B.
Delivery: 1920x1080 clips of the exact cue spans (A 13.9 s, B 13.6 s), for the Battle Map v2 re-direct of Agincourt
(the film's join step places them over those spans).
