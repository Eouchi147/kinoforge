# Sample trade ad: drainage and landscaping (Cutaway style)

Purpose: show Unmissed's trade clients (landscaping, drainage, pools, plumbing, foundations) an ad no local competitor
has: the cutaway shows the work the customer never sees. This one is a sample for an invented business.

- Business (invented, marked "Sample" on screen): **Fieldstone Drainage and Landscaping**. No phone number, no website:
  the end card says "Your logo here" in small type under the sample name, so nobody mistakes it for a real company.
- Length 15 s; deliver 1080x1920 (Reels, TikTok, Shorts) and 1920x1080. Sound off works: every beat reads from the
  picture and the words; music from the free score engine (a light, warm loop with one lift at the fix).
- Words set in code, one face, big, inside the social safe box (x 0.11 to 0.78, y 0.15 to 0.65 of the 9:16 frame,
  rule R-2-08).

| Time | Picture (one idea per beat) | Words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.0 to 3.0 | a backyard block after rain: a puddle on the lawn; the cut face shows water trapped above heavy grey clay | Soggy lawn after every rain? |
| 3.0 to 6.5 | slow push to the cut face: the water sits on the clay and has nowhere to go | The problem is under the grass. |
| 6.5 to 11.5 | a trench opens along the yard, gravel pours in, a perforated pipe settles in it, soil and turf close over; the water drains into the pipe and away (the music lifts) | We fix what you can't see. |
| 11.5 to 15.0 | the same block in sun, the lawn dry and green; end card: sample name, "Sample", "Book a yard check" | Fieldstone Drainage and Landscaping / Book a yard check |

Every move: one slow push or orbit per beat, eased; the fix (beat 3) is the only fast-feeling moment, and it is fast
because the work is shown compressed, which the viewer reads as a time-lapse (a small clock icon is not needed).
