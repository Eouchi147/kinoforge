# Social loop: four seasons over one block (Cutaway style)

12 s, 1080x1920, a seamless loop (the last frame flows into the first). One block, one slow continuous orbit of 90
degrees per loop (the block is square, so the turn hides the seam), the seasons passing:

| Time | Season | What changes (each change has a reason we can see) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to 3 s | spring | blossom on the trees, fresh grass, the pond clear, a few birds |
| 3 to 6 s | summer | full green, the pond warm and bright, insects over the water |
| 6 to 9 s | autumn | leaves turn and fall onto the pond and sink through the cut face, the grass browns |
| 9 to 12 s | winter | snow settles, the pond freezes from the edges (ice visible in the cut face), then thaws into spring |

Underground stays alive: roots grow a little in spring, a hibernating frog in the mud in winter (seen in the cut face),
which is the small surprise that makes people watch it twice. Sound: wind, birds, a short loopable warm score.
